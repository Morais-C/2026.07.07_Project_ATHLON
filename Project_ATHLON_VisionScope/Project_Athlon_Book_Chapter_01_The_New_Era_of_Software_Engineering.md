# Chapter 1 --- The New Era of Software Engineering

## Introduction

Software engineering has gone through several major eras:

1.  Hand-written software
2.  Framework-driven development
3.  Cloud-native engineering
4.  AI-assisted development
5.  Agentic software engineering

The first generation of AI tools focused on code completion and
chat-based assistance. While these tools significantly improved
developer productivity, they largely operated as individual assistants.

The next evolution is **Agentic Software Engineering**: a coordinated
team of specialized AI agents working together across the entire
Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC), with humans providing
governance, priorities, and final approval.

## Why this matters

Software delivery involves much more than writing code. Requirements,
architecture, testing, documentation, security, deployment, and
maintenance all consume engineering effort. Project Athlon explores how
AI can assist across the complete lifecycle rather than only at
implementation time.

## Principles of the Agentic Era

-   Specialization over one general-purpose assistant.
-   Structured artifacts instead of free-form conversations.
-   Human approval for significant decisions.
-   Traceability from business request to deployment.
-   Continuous evaluation of quality and safety.

## Challenges

-   Consistent context across agents.
-   Reliable structured outputs.
-   Governance and auditability.
-   Cost and latency.
-   Integration with existing engineering tools.
